I would make mentorship an upgrade from the $250 service rather than creating a completely separate app.

# 23. Important financial/regulatory consideration

Because Wick Betts will charge people for **stock/crypto signals and mentorship**, this needs to be addressed **before launch**, not after the app is built.

The SEC describes an investment adviser broadly around providing advice about securities for compensation as part of a business, and it specifically discusses compensated advice/analysis and investment recommendations. ([SEC][1])

That means the exact way Wick Betts provides signals and mentorship could have regulatory implications depending on what you recommend, how personalized the advice becomes, who you serve, and how the business is structured.

The marketing side matters too. The SEC's current guidance covers claims, performance information, testimonials/endorsements, and recordkeeping for investment-adviser marketing. ([SEC][2])

**I would therefore put a securities/financial-regulatory attorney review into Phase 1 of the project.** The app shouldn't launch with claims such as guaranteed returns, "never lose," or unsupported performance claims.
